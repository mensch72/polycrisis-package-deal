## What to send

1. A short personal email. Drafts are in `outreach/email_A.md` and `outreach/email_B.md`.
2. The two-page brief for the track: `outreach/brief_A.md` or `outreach/brief_B.md`.
3. Nothing else at first. The design note follows on request.

Not to send: the repository while it is private, the toy write-ups, or any number about a named actor
that is not marked as a placeholder.

## Channel

1. Email from Jobst's institutional address, to one person at a time, starting with anyone he knows.
2. Where he knows nobody, an introduction through a colleague beats a cold message.
3. For track B, researchers before companies. After the class action of September 2026, a message that
   looks like an attempt to convene developers would be unwelcome and possibly unwise.
4. No public posting until the theory note is on arXiv and Jobst has decided on the repository.

## Risks

1. **Track B and competition law.** The project itself must not become a channel between developers.
   Jobst talks to each party separately, and to lawyers first.
2. **Track A and politics.** The South African partnership is contested at home. The approach is research,
   through researchers.
3. **Claude's tie to Anthropic** in track B. Disclosed in the brief; an independent reader should check the framing.
4. **Over-claiming.** Both skeletons rest on invented preferences. The briefs say so in each paragraph that uses them.
