# Track A: verified public facts for the pilot registry design note

Retrieved 10 November 2026. Every row carries the URL fetched and a verbatim quote of under 40 words. Each quote was machine-checked against the saved page text (whitespace normalised). Values are my reading of the source; the quote is the evidence. Nothing is filled from memory.

Notes on sources.
- European Commission press releases are script-rendered, so they were read through the press-corner API; the URL given is the API call. The human-readable page is `https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/<ref>` (ip_21_5768, ip_22_6926, statement_22_6892, ip_23_3448).
- Quotes from PDF tables are flattened rows; the column order is stated in the claim cell.
- Section 2 comes from the Climate Watch API (WRI). Its text fields are WRI summaries or WRI translations, not always NDC wording; rows say so. Raw JSON is in `trackA_raw/`. NDC documents themselves were not opened: the UNFCCC registry page returned an empty body to a non-browser client.
- "Secondary" in a claim cell marks a non-primary source.

JET PMU, 30 June 2026: Table 3 grand total 13,177 pledged and 7,276 allocated (USD mn), while the report text gives an "inclusive" total of USD 14.382bn including private-sector and local DFI commitments.
